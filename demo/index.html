Reconciliation Canvas

An upper tree (species, hosts, geography) drawn as fat tubes, with lower trees (genes, symbionts) threaded inside. Time runs down the page. Where a lower tree stops following its tube, the events that explain it are drawn: duplication, loss, horizontal transfer.

Drag to pan · scroll to zoom at the cursor · double-click a clade to fit it · hover anything for details. Everything here is simulated: the gene trees are grown along the species tree with a duplication–transfer–loss process, then pruned to what a reconciliation could actually observe.
